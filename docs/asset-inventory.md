## What the game uses instead (all procedural, all original, no licence restrictions)

| Need | Procedural replacement | Where | Trade-off vs. a authored asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain | Analytic height function (seeded simplex fBm + ridged noise), streamed as a quadtree of LOD meshes with skirts | `src/world.js`, `src/terrain.js` | No sculpted features or erosion; distant mountains are coarser than a hand-made heightmap. |
| Ground texture | Tileable value-noise detail map, sampled at two scales, multiplied over per-vertex colours | `src/textures.js` | No photographic soil or pebble detail. |
| Grass | Instanced tapered blades + pampas-plume geometry, shader-animated wind | `src/grass.js` | Blades are flat cards with no alpha textures; very close up they read as ribbons. |
| Rocks / pebbles | Displaced icosahedra with faceting, crevice darkening and moss on top faces | `src/prop-geometry.js` | Smooth-ish shapes without scanned detail. |
| Trees | Japanese black pine (tube trunk + foliage pads) and broadleaf (lumpy lobes) | `src/prop-geometry.js` | Stylised clump foliage, no leaf cards. |
| Landmarks | Torii gate, stone lantern, wooden posts | `src/prop-geometry.js` | Simple primitives. |
| Distant mountains | Three ridgeline rings with per-layer haze | `src/mountains.js` | Silhouettes only. |
| Sky | Shader dome: gradient, sun glow, sun disc, fBm clouds | `src/atmosphere.js` | Static time of day, 2D clouds. |
| Samurai model | Hierarchy of lathe/capsule/cylinder meshes: hat, head, kimono, armour plates, hakama, sandals, swords, simulated cloak | `src/character.js` | Not a sculpted, skinned mesh; joints are rigid segments, so there is no skin deformation and close-ups look stylised. |
| Animation | Procedural pose blending (idle breathing, walk, run, jump, fall, land, turn lean), 2-bone IK for the left hand on the katana, spring-driven cloak | `src/animation.js` | See limitations below. |
| Fabric / straw textures | Procedural weave patterns | `src/textures.js` | — |
| Ambient audio | Web Audio synthesis: pink/brown-noise wind layers tied to the visual gusts, grass rustle, footsteps, synthesised bird calls, convolution reverb | `src/audio.js` | Synthesised birds are simpler than recordings. |

## Animation limitations (procedural fallback)

- No motion-captured or keyframed animation set was available; all motion is computed from parametric poses.
- No foot IK: on steep slopes feet can sink slightly into or hover above the ground (usually hidden by grass).
- Rigid segments: elbows, knees and the hakama can show small gaps or interpenetration at extreme poses.
- The cloak is a 1-D chain of hanging rows, not a full cloth simulation, and does not collide with the body (it is kept clear of the legs by a constraint).
- Turning is a rotation of the whole body with lean; there are no dedicated turn-in-place or stop animations.
